BEST BAR v1.2.3 - CONDITIONAL GOLD + DIAMOND MARKER FIX

1. The real top progress bar stays normal before the saved best.

5. The saved-best marker is larger and centered directly on the stock bar instead of floating above it.
6. The Gold Run pause button matches the Practice button size and anchors beside the stock retry/practice row at the same Y level, avoiding overlap.
