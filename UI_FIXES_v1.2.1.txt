BEST BAR v1.2.1 - UI FIXES

This update directly addresses the screenshots from v1.2.0:

1. The mod logo was completely rebuilt for small Geode card display.
2. The saved-best bar is no longer overlaid in the center of the stock bar.
3. The saved-best bar now sits underneath, is shorter, thicker, outlined, glowing, and clearly gold.
4. The actual current-progress fill gets a gold overlay after the attempt reaches the previous best.
5. Sparkles, shine sweeps, flashes, and star bursts are larger and more visible.
6. The Gold Run button is now a 108-unit button instead of the tiny 0.27-scale icon.
7. The Gold Run icon, glow, ACTIVE/RUN labels, lock, and pause-menu placement were redesigned.
